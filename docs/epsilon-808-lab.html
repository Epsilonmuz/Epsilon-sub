808 Lab

The Epsilon 808 engine running in your browser: same oscillator, punch, glide, drive, Translate, Glue and Phone Check as the plugin. Start the audio, then play the keys or run the trap pattern.

Click the keys, or type A W S E D F… with Z / X to change octave (C1). Hold a note and press another to hear the glide. Drag knobs up and down, double-click to reset.

Make it heard on phones

Turns Phone Check on with Translate at 0. The 808 almost disappears. Then Translate goes to 70% and the line comes back.

Four drive flavours

Cycles Tape, Tube, Hard and Fold at +18 dB, two bars each, over the pattern.

Glide styles

Legato slides only where notes overlap. Always slides into every note, the drill sound.

Kick Lock

One pass of the loop with Kick Lock off, then one with it on: the 808 snaps into phase with the kick and ducks under it. Listen to the low end tighten.

Split drive

One pass with heavy Hard drive and Split off, then Split at 110 Hz: the grit stays, the sub comes back clean.

Punch and knock

Punch 0 is a soft sine. Punch 24 with a short Punch Time is the knock that cuts through a kick.

The pattern is four bars at 140 BPM in C minor (Cm7, Abmaj7, Fm7, G7). All presets are loudness-matched, as in the plugin. Phone Check here filters the whole beat, so you hear the 808 against the drums the way a phone plays them; in the plugin it filters the 808 track. The plugin adds user presets, A/B slots, undo, a loudness meter, pitch-bend and host automation.
