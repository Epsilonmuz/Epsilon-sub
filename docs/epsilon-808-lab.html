808 Lab

The Epsilon 808 v1.1 engine running in your browser: the same engine, sample layers, Phase Lock and 808 character as the plugin. Start the audio, then play the keys or run the trap pattern. For the hybrid sounds, drop your sound pack on the HYBRID page.

Click the keys, or type A W S E D F… with Z / X to change octave (C1). Hold a note and press another to hear the glide. Drag knobs up and down, double-click to reset.

Phase Lock

Heavyweight Hybrid with the full sample under the engine. One pass with Phase Lock off (the lows fight and thin out), one with it on (they add up). Needs your sound pack.

808 character

808 Classic with Snap, Sigh, Vary, Vel > Length and Tone Decay at 0 (the machine), then back on (the hardware).

Hybrid vs engine

Layer Cake as the engine alone, then with its two layers: the Heavyweight front handing its tail to the Punchy reese. Needs your sound pack.

Make it heard on phones

Turns Phone Check on with Translate at 0. The 808 almost disappears. Then Translate goes to 70% and the line comes back.

Four drive flavours

Cycles Tape, Tube, Hard and Fold at +18 dB, two bars each, over the pattern.

Glide styles

Legato slides only where notes overlap. Always slides into every note, the drill sound.

Kick Lock

One pass of the loop with Kick Lock off, then one with it on: the 808 snaps into phase with the kick and ducks under it. Listen to the low end tighten.

Split drive

One pass with heavy Hard drive and Split off, then Split at 110 Hz: the grit stays, the sub comes back clean.

Punch and knock

Punch 0 is a soft sine. Punch 24 with a short Punch Time is the knock that cuts through a kick.

The pattern is four bars at 140 BPM in C minor (Cm7, Abmaj7, Fm7, G7). All presets are loudness-matched, as in the plugin. Phone Check here filters the whole beat, so you hear the 808 against the drums the way a phone plays them; in the plugin it filters the 808 track. The plugin adds user presets, A/B slots, undo, a loudness meter, pitch-bend and host automation. Sounds you drop here stay in this browser only; nothing is uploaded.
